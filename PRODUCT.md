# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operational staff, internal support users, and engineering users who manage API-driven tasks, schedules, manual runs, and execution visibility inside the organization. They use the interface during active operational work, so the product needs to stay readable, fast to scan, and dependable under routine monitoring and troubleshooting.

## Product Purpose

TaskScheduler centralizes the management of internal API-based tasks and schedules. The product should help operators review task status, edit configurations safely, move between steps, schedules, and logs without losing context, and diagnose failures quickly with a predictable workflow.

## Brand Personality

Clean, operational, and dependable. The tone should feel precise and calm, with expert confidence instead of marketing energy or consumer-style polish.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful visual language, oversized cards, consumer-app styling, decorative gradients, and layouts that prioritize spectacle over task flow. Avoid split-workspace patterns that make dense operational actions feel cramped or indirect.

## Design Principles

1. Keep operators in flow: navigation and page structure should reduce context switching and make the next action obvious.
2. Prefer dense clarity over decorative space: show the right operational context, not oversized chrome.
3. Make structure predictable: repeated actions, forms, grids, and logs should follow the same visual vocabulary throughout the app.
4. Surface status without drama: state, risk, and execution outcomes should be visible immediately, but never noisy.
5. Preserve trust through restraint: motion, color, and emphasis should support decision-making, not compete with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Treat WCAG AA as the target baseline for the React admin UI. Preserve keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion-friendly interactions. Avoid relying on color alone for status or meaning.
